Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty **"Make the AI Judge Laugh: Cat Reviews Its Human"** — a one-star review written by a house cat about its human, under 200 characters, judged head to head by an AI judge.

## The review

> One star. He talks to me in a baby voice, then wonders why I don't respect him. I knocked his laptop off the table, and he apologised to me.

(140 characters, one line.)

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network) writes the review to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
